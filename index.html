How to cast
  1. Print both shell halves and the base plate (PLA/PETG, 3+ walls). Print the master too if you don't have it.
  2. Glue the master into the matching footprint pocket in the base plate (hot glue or CA).
  3. Bolt the halves together (M3 by default) around the master, seat them inside the ridge and seal the seams with hot glue or plasticine.
  4. Spray mould release if your silicone needs it. Mix and degas the silicone, then pour slowly in a thin stream into the funnel until it reaches the vents and the funnel.
  5. After curing, unbolt the shell, cut the silicone along one side if needed to release the master. Put the silicone back in the shell for support when casting resin through the bottom opening.
